# course-planner

Plan your Suffolk Law semesters. It runs on the LITSA site at **suffolklitsa.org/planner**; this repo holds its code and data pipeline.

**Version 1 (before spring 2027 registration in November):** pick spring sections and see time conflicts, exam-date conflicts, missing prerequisites and your credit total.

**Version 2 (before April registration):** a graduation check against the JD requirements and the LIT concentration, plus a career-path survey that suggests practice areas, courses, clinics and faculty.

This is an unofficial student tool. Workday and the Registrar are the official record.

## Privacy

Everything runs in your browser. Nothing you enter is sent to LITSA or stored anywhere. We count page visits with cookieless analytics and nothing else.

## Data

Course sections, meeting times, prerequisites, block and exam schedules come from Suffolk's public pages. The Suffolk assistant re-checks those pages weekly; this repo reads the same files rather than scraping them again.
