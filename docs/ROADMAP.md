# Roadmap

## Version 1: spring schedule builder (before November registration)

- [x] Course data importer and data contract, twice-daily refresh
- [x] Search (including descriptions), sort, filters (division, format, area, credits, requirements, days, times)
- [x] One card per course; profile (year, division); taken and not interested
- [x] Plan A and B, shortlist, conflicts in results, prerequisites, exam conflicts, credit and online checks, requirement tally
- [x] Week view, detail panel, Workday list, share link, calendar export, print view
- [x] Data freshness note, disappearing-section warning, report-a-problem links
- [ ] Sam's review on staging, then a week of testing with LITSA members
- [ ] Release `v0.1.0` (puts it on suffolklitsa.org/planner) and announce it

## Version 2: graduation check and career-path survey (before April registration)

- JD requirements written as a checklist with sources, reviewed by Research & Writing
- Prerequisite parsing and warnings
- Exam conflict check once Suffolk posts the spring exam schedule
- Plan multiple semesters; what-if scenarios
- Concentration tracking, starting with LIT
- Career-path survey:
  - About 22 practice areas with sub-areas, anchored to the ABA and Massachusetts Bar Association sections and Suffolk's own Areas of Focus
  - Interest model adapted from the public-domain O*NET Interest Profiler
  - Branching scenario questions with a stopping rule, short "try the work" tasks, and an explanation before any unfamiliar area
  - Results rank every area, explain why, and link to an area page (a real day, real public work product, clients, entry paths, Suffolk courses and clinics, faculty from public pages, pay ranges with sources)
  - Rules-based and editable; answers stay in the browser; anonymous sharing only by opt-in
  - Optional resume reading in the browser to prefill answers
- Tested in rounds of 5 to 8 students, LITSA members and others
